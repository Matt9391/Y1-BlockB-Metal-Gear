Detailed Description

Draws the 2D game world: everything that lives in the level and moves when the camera moves.

Owned by buz::game_2d and handed to on_render_world every frame. Until you call set_camera, world positions are plain window pixels, with (0, 0) in the top-left corner. A camera is two matrices: the view (where the camera is) and the projection (how the world maps onto the window). Scrolling a level to follow the player is a view that moves:

const buz::mat4 view = glm::translate(buz::mat4(1.0f), buz::vec3(-scroll_x, 0.0f, 0.0f));
const buz::mat4 projection = buz::make_orthographic_projection(1280.0f, 720.0f);
world.set_camera(view, projection);
